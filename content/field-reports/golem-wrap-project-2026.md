**Launch Date:** 24 October 2026  
**Location:** GOLEM Owode Mission House, Owode, Western Nigeria  
**Project:** WRAP — Widow Relief Assisted Project  
**Organisation:** God of Love Emancipation Ministries (GOLEM)

### 1. Successful Project Launch

The GOLEM Widow Relief Assisted Project (WRAP) was successfully launched on 24 October 2026 at the GOLEM Owode Mission House, as planned.

As part of the launch, food packages were distributed to *10 eligible widows and vulnerable individuals who met the project's needy-person criteria*. This initial distribution marked an important milestone in GOLEM's commitment to providing practical assistance to struggling individuals and families experiencing hardship.

The launch demonstrated the project's purpose: to provide essential food support to people who need it most, offering practical relief and a measure of reassurance to vulnerable members of the community.

### 2. WRAP Is Now Operational

Following its successful launch, the WRAP Project is now live and running daily from the GOLEM Owode Mission House.

The programme is intended to provide a vital lifeline for struggling families by helping address their immediate food needs. Rather than being a one-off charitable event, WRAP has been established as an ongoing initiative through which GOLEM seeks to provide continued practical support to eligible people facing hardship.

The successful launch represents the beginning of this continuing mission of compassion, dignity and service.

### 3. The Need for Grants, Donations and Partners' Support

To sustain daily operations, GOLEM must regularly replenish food stocks as supplies are distributed to beneficiaries.

*Continued financial support is therefore essential to keep the WRAP Project operating throughout the year.* GOLEM welcomes grants, individual donations, church contributions, corporate support and partnerships with organisations that share its commitment to relieving poverty and supporting vulnerable people.

Ongoing contributions will help GOLEM replenish essential food supplies, maintain the project's operations and continue providing support to struggling families who meet its eligibility criteria.

GOLEM invites prospective donors and partners to join this initiative and help ensure that the project can continue responding to essential food needs in the community.

### 4. Conclusion: Love in Action

The successful launch of the WRAP Project on 24 October 2026 marks an important step in GOLEM's continuing work to support widows and vulnerable individuals.

With 10 eligible beneficiaries receiving food packages at the launch, and daily operations now underway at the GOLEM Owode Mission House, the project has moved from planning into active service.

However, sustaining this vital lifeline requires continuing grants, donations and partners' support to replenish depleted food stocks and maintain the programme throughout the year.

Together, we can help ensure that struggling families receive practical assistance, compassion and hope when they need them most.

**GOLEM — LOVE IN ACTION.**

Supporting widows, vulnerable individuals and struggling families through practical compassion.
